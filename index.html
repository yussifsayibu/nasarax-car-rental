FAQ / Answers

Good questions
deserve clear answers.

Which payment methods do you accept?

This front-end demo does not process real payments. A production version can connect to your preferred payment provider.

Is there a mileage limit?

Most rentals in the concept include unlimited mileage. Individual vehicles can have their own terms shown before booking.

Can I travel between cities?

Yes. Intercity travel can be requested during booking, with any additional requirements confirmed by the team.

Can I make a special request?

Yes. Add a note during reservation or use the special request form in the client area.

Is this a real booking platform?

This portfolio version is a front-end demo. Login and booking actions are simulated locally and do not create real reservations.

Case study / UI direction

From rental website
to mobility product.

A visual and UX direction focused on clearer hierarchy, faster booking and a stronger premium identity.

Problem

Too much interface, not enough direction.

The original experience relied heavily on boxes, small type and a conventional rental-site structure.

Direction

A calmer, more editorial interface.

Large type, controlled contrast, strong imagery and focused actions create a more current product feel.

System

One visual language across every state.

Public pages, client dashboard and booking flow share the same spacing, typography, states and interaction patterns.

Outcome

Fewer decisions per screen.

The redesign turns browsing and booking into a guided journey rather than a collection of forms.
